The Challenge
Products shouldn't lose value between production and delivery.
Africa's temperature-sensitive economy depends on a cold chain that is often fragmented, expensive and energy-intensive.
Businesses face product spoilage, unreliable power, diesel-dependent refrigeration, limited temperature visibility and the high cost of owning specialized cold-chain infrastructure.
Dara is changing how that infrastructure works.
We are bringing together clean energy, intelligent refrigeration, electric mobility, IoT and logistics technology into a connected cold-chain ecosystem.

The Dara Climate-Tech Ecosystem
One connected system for moving, cooling and monitoring products.
☀️ Solar-Powered Refrigeration
Keep products cold using the power of the sun.
Dara is integrating solar-powered refrigeration and cold-storage systems into its cold-chain infrastructure.
Solar energy can power refrigeration and support cold-storage operations, reducing dependence on diesel generators and unreliable grid electricity.
This enables Dara to progressively build distributed cold infrastructure closer to farms, production centres, warehouses, markets and distribution zones.
The opportunity:
Solar Energy → Refrigeration → Product Preservation → Reduced Waste

⚡ Electric Refrigerated Vans & Trucks
Cleaner movement across our delivery zones.
Dara's climate mobility strategy includes the adoption of electric refrigerated vans and trucks for temperature-controlled shipments across suitable delivery zones.
Electric vehicles can support first-mile, last-mile and selected distribution routes while reducing reliance on conventional internal-combustion vehicles.
Our vision is to progressively integrate:
Electric Refrigerated Vans
For urban and last-mile temperature-controlled deliveries.
Electric Refrigerated Trucks
For larger-volume shipments across suitable distribution corridors.
Charging Infrastructure
Integrated strategically within Dara's logistics and cold-storage network.
From cold storage to delivery, we are working toward a cleaner movement of temperature-sensitive products.

❄️ Smart Cold Storage
Cold infrastructure where businesses need it.
Dara's distributed cold-storage strategy brings temperature-controlled infrastructure closer to the points where products are produced, processed, distributed and consumed.
By combining solar refrigeration, smart energy systems and digital monitoring, these facilities can help businesses preserve products while reducing dependence on conventional diesel-powered cooling.

This creates greater visibility and a digital record of temperature-sensitive shipments.

05 — Deliver
Products arrive at their destination with greater visibility and temperature control.
